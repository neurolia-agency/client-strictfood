Lighting: warm overhead spots as key light hitting the top of the tray, the fists, and the burger's top bun. Specular highlights on the nitrile glove knuckles and the Coca-Cola can. Natural contact shadow under the tray. The fist bump point of contact shows compression on the glove material.

The overall feel is documentary-style food photography — real, slightly imperfect, authentic, NOT a stock photo. Slight natural film grain. The burger must look ultra-realistic and appetizing, with rich saturated ingredient colors popping against the dark bun. Portrait 9:16 format. Preserve the exact composition, framing, and energy of the reference image — the fist bump over the tray IS the shot.
