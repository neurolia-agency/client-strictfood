# Prompt — Story Rappel Paques Bun Noir (05-04-2026)

## Mode
full-ia (brand/rappel) — fond charbon — concept brand-bun-noir (variante Paques)

## Prompt Gemini (Combo-B)

Intimate food photography, single charcoal black sesame bun nestled in a rustic straw nest, as if it were a precious Easter egg just discovered. Slight overhead angle, 3/4 plunge, portrait orientation 9:16.

The bun sits slightly tilted in the hollow of the nest, not perfectly centered — like a real egg. A few straw strands climb up and partially overlap the base of the bun. Charcoal black sesame bun — deep ink-dark surface, densely covered with golden sesame seeds in RANDOM uneven distribution. Artisanal imperfections: visible cracks in the crust, slightly asymmetric shape, one side slightly more puffed, micro-fissures where the dark dough peeks through, rustic handmade quality.

The nest is made of real golden dry straw and hay, tightly woven but imperfect — the kind you would see in an artisan bakery window display at Easter, or in a farmers market basket. Warm golden tones in the straw. A few loose sesame seeds scattered on the dark surface around the nest, as if the egg had been placed there moments ago.

The surface beneath is dark rough wood or slate — visible texture, not pure black void. Grounded, realistic, like a kitchen counter or a rustic table.

Single warm directional side light from the left, like a kitchen window — soft but sculpted. The sesame seeds catch the light and glow like tiny golden sparks. The straw nest is naturally lit in amber tones — organic match with the StrictFood palette. Subtle warm shadows on the right side of the bun.

Tight framing on the bun and nest, no wide context. Shallow depth of field, f/2.8, 85mm lens. The bun and center of the nest are tack-sharp, the nest edges and background fall into soft bokeh. Natural film grain, warm tone shift.

No text, no logos, no people, no hands. Documentary food photography.
