## Prompt (Combo-B, 248 mots)

```
Shot on Canon EOS R5, 85mm f/1.8, ISO 200. Documentary food photography.

A single chicken burger resting on a warm amber-gold textured surface — aged kraft paper with subtle creases and golden sand-like warmth. The burger sits slightly off-center to the left, viewed from a 3/4 angle with a gentle low-angle perspective. Golden hour light pours in from the right side, casting long velvety shadows stretching left across the surface.

The charcoal black sesame bun — deep ink-dark surface, pitch-black, densely covered with golden sesame seeds in RANDOM uneven distribution. Artisanal imperfections: visible cracks in the crust, slightly asymmetric shape, one side more puffed than the other, micro-fissures, rustic handmade quality. Each sesame seed catches the golden light, glowing like tiny amber jewels.

Between the buns: deep golden-brown Maillard crust chicken slices (oven-seared at high pulsed heat) — caramelized surface with crispy edges and darker spots at thinnest points, shredded pale meat fibers visible at torn edges. Finely grated parmesan dust — tiny powdery granules scattered like sand across the chicken. Thin-sliced red onion rings with visible concentric layers. Lamb's lettuce (mâche) — three small round spoon-shaped whole leaves peeking from the edge, one slightly wilted. A thin delicate drizzle of yellow-orange pepper sauce — a single fine thread trailing down the front.

The burger naturally sags under its own weight. The bun cap tilts slightly. A few sesame seeds have fallen onto the kraft surface beside the burger.

Warm amber bokeh in the background with soft circular highlights. Kodak Gold 200 film grain. Rich golden tones throughout. Deep contrast between the pitch-black bun and warm amber surroundings.

Vertical 9:16 composition. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no people, no hands.
```
