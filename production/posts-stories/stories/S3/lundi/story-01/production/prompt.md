# Prompt — Story 01 (full-ia macro-sauce)

## Mode
`full-ia` — Gemini 4K, 9:16

## Concept visuel
`macro-sauce` — Micro-saison A (Macro & Texture)

## Prompt

Extreme close-up macro photograph of a homemade pepper sauce slowly dripping down a black sesame bun. The sauce is the hero subject — yellow-orange color, viscous, each droplet and thread of sauce captured with surgical sharpness. The black sesame bun serves as a dark textured backdrop, individual golden sesame seeds visible. Shot on Hasselblad H6D, 120mm macro f/2.8, ISO 200. Dramatic side lighting at 45 degrees from the left, revealing every droplet and amber reflection. Ultra-dark charcoal background. Slight bokeh on the bun edges. Warm copper/amber tones on sauce reflections. No hands, no context — just sauce and bun. Style: precious object in darkness. Portrait orientation 9:16. Hyper-realistic food photography, not illustration.

## Contraintes recette
- Sauce poivron = jaune-orange subtil (PAS rouge, PAS blanche)
- Pain noir sesame obligatoire (black bun)
- Pas de mains, pas de contexte

## Resolution
4K
