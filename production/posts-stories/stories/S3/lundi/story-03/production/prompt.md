# Prompt — Story 03 (full-ia sensation-goutte)

## Mode
`full-ia` — Gemini 4K, 9:16

## Concept visuel
`sensation-goutte` — Micro-saison A (Macro & Texture)

## Prompt

Extreme macro photograph of a single chicken tender held by a black-gloved hand. The tender has a hyper-detailed golden crispy breading crust — every grain, ridge and crackle visible. At the tip of the tender, ONE SINGLE perfect sauce droplet is suspended in mid-air — time frozen. The droplet is round, slightly translucent, with amber reflections and visible surface tension. Shot on Canon EOS R5, 100mm macro f/2.8, ISO 400. Single spotlight from top-right, everything else in deep shadow. The black-gloved hand disappears into the charcoal background — only the tender and the droplet are visible. Ultra-dark background. The crispy crust is backlit revealing suspended crumbs. Portrait orientation 9:16. Hyper-realistic food photography, not illustration.

- Cuisson chaleur pulsee (pas de friture)
- PAS de burger, PAS de pain noir (c'est un snack)
