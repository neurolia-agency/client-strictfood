## Prompt final

Real food photograph. A SINGLE CONTINUOUS ultra-wide 16:9 dark food scene — ONE unbroken image, NOT a collage, NOT multiple frames. Phase One IQ4 150MP, 80mm lens at f/4, natural film grain. A CONTINUOUS DARK TEXTURED SLATE SURFACE extends seamlessly from the far left edge to the far right edge — one unbroken matte black stone countertop, subtle warm reflections running across its width, scattered golden sesame seeds and tiny crumbs RANDOMLY distributed creating lived-in texture.

LEFT TO CENTER: the hero — a tall double-chicken burger in dramatic three-quarter profile on a sheet of crumpled kraft paper. From bottom to top: charcoal black sesame bun base — deep ink-dark surface with artisanal imperfections: visible CRACKS in the crust like dried earth, slightly asymmetric shape, one side more puffed, micro-FISSURES, golden sesame seeds in RANDOM uneven distribution. Sitting directly on the bun: DOUBLE LAYER of thin irregular roasted chicken slices (each 5-8cm long) with deep golden-brown Maillard crust (oven-seared at high pulsed heat, NOT breaded, NOT battered, NO panko — each slice has a caramelized surface with visible crispy edges, deep golden-brown coloring with darker caramelized spots at the thinnest points, shredded pale meat fibers visible at torn edges, the surface has a rough seared grain NOT smooth NOT pale NOT raw-looking — INTENSE sear on tender chicken), juices pooling on the surface. The two chicken layers are NOT perfectly aligned — the top layer shifted 1-2cm to one side, offset like placed by hand. On top of the chicken: finely grated parmesan DUST scattered like sand — tiny powdery granules (NOT shavings, NOT slices). Above: mache leaves — small round spoon-shaped whole leaves with visible vein texture, varied sizes, one crumpled, one folded (NOT arugula, NEVER uniform). Thin-sliced RED ONION RINGS with concentric layers. Yellow-orange pepper sauce TRAILING DOWN from between the top bun and toppings in 1-2 thin glossy threads like warm honey. Top bun cap: charcoal black with visible cracks, one side slightly LOPSIDED, a flat spot where it settled, sesame in uneven clusters — NOT a pristine dome. The bun is COMPACT, slightly compressed, ingredients BULGING OUT 2-3cm past the edges. A small BLACK RAMEKIN of yellow-orange pepper sauce beside the burger.

A few stray frites scattered on the bare slate BETWEEN the burger and the fries — they visually CONNECT the two elements, proving this is one continuous scene.

RIGHT THIRD: sweet potato fries FANNING OUT of a crumpled kraft paper cone lying on its side. Surfaces deeply CARAMELIZED orange, air-fried with crispy edges. Some fries spill toward the center.

Single warm directional key light from FRONT-LEFT (8 o'clock position, slightly elevated), hitting the FRONT FACE of the burger head-on — the entire front profile of the bun, chicken layers, and ingredients is FULLY LIT and luminous. 3200K throughout the ENTIRE frame. Shadows fall BEHIND and to the right of the food, NOT across the front face. Specular highlights on sauce trails, chicken sear, sesame seeds, all facing the camera. SMOOTH CONTINUOUS lighting gradient — no brightness jumps. The burger POPS luminously against the dark background — rich saturated ingredient colors.

NO text. NO logos. NO grill marks. NO white bun. NO arugula. NO ketchup. NO borders. NO frames. NO collage. NO separate sections. NO breaded chicken. NO panko. Aspect ratio: 16:9.
