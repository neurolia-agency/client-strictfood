## Slide 1 — Plan large

```
Documentary food photography. A TALL DOUBLE charcoal black sesame burger sits on a dark slate surface with subtle grain texture. The bun is pitch-black with deep ink-dark surface densely covered with golden sesame seeds in RANDOM uneven distribution, artisanal imperfections: visible cracks in the crust, slightly asymmetric shape, micro-fissures, rustic handmade quality. Inside: TWO PORTIONS of thin irregular roasted chicken slices (each 5-8cm) with deep golden-brown Maillard crust (oven-seared at high pulsed heat, caramelized surface with crispy edges, darker spots at thinnest points, shredded pale meat fibers at torn edges, NOT grilled, NOT smooth) stacked DIRECTLY on top of each other with sauce and parmesan as the ONLY separators — NO middle bun. Mâche leaves (small round spoon-shaped whole leaves, varied sizes, one crumpled, one folded, NOT arugula), thin-sliced red onion rings with visible concentric layers, finely grated parmesan dust (tiny powdery granules like sand, NOT shavings), a thin delicate drizzle of yellow-orange pepper sauce. The burger sits on crumpled kraft paper. Scattered sesame seeds and parmesan crumbs on the slate. Shot from 3/4 above (~40 degrees), the burger occupies ~55% of the frame with breathing room around it. Single warm directional light from upper-left (10 o'clock), 3200K, dramatic shadows falling right. Dark matte background. No text, no logos, no people. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Slide 2 — Plan serre

```
Documentary food photography, close-up. A TALL DOUBLE charcoal black sesame burger fills the frame. The pitch-black bun dominates the top — deep ink-dark surface, golden sesame seeds in random uneven distribution, artisanal cracks and micro-fissures. Viewed from the SIDE, every layer visible in profile: bottom black bun, FIRST PORTION of thin irregular roasted chicken slices with deep golden-brown Maillard crust (oven-seared at high pulsed heat, caramelized crispy edges, darker spots, shredded pale fibers at torn edges), yellow-orange pepper sauce layer BETWEEN portions (glossy, thick), SECOND PORTION of chicken stacked DIRECTLY on the first — NO middle bun between them, sauce and parmesan as ONLY separators. The double stack is impressively tall. Mâche leaves (small round spoon-shaped, varied sizes, imperfections) bursting past the bun edge. Red onion thin rings displaced. Parmesan dust (powdery granules, NOT shavings) stuck to sauce. A thick drip of yellow-orange sauce trailing down the side of the burger toward the dark slate surface. Shallow depth of field — background softening. Single warm directional light from upper-left, 3200K, highlights on sauce gloss and sesame seeds. Dark matte background. No text, no logos, no people. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Slide 3 — Macro

```
Macro food photograph. Extreme close-up focusing on THE ZONE BETWEEN THE TWO CHICKEN PORTIONS inside a charcoal black sesame burger. Both portions' Maillard crusts visible in hyper-detail — deep golden-brown caramelized surfaces, darker spots at thinnest points, shredded pale meat fibers at torn edges, juice beads glistening in the crevasses of the rough crust. BETWEEN the two portions: yellow-orange pepper sauce THICK and VISCOUS, POOLING and OOZING out like warm honey, glossy and brilliant. Finely grated parmesan dust scattered across the sauce — tiny powdery granules, some intact and sandy-textured, others slightly softened at contact with warm chicken (but still GRANULAR, NOT melted, NOT stretchy). A crumpled mâche leaf edge barely visible, sauce-coated. The black sesame bun a golden blur in the background. Shallow depth of field — only the between-portions zone razor-sharp (~3cm in focus). Soft warm light from upper-left, creating gentle rim glow on chicken edges and translucent sauce highlights. Dark matte background. No text, no logos, no people. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Slide 4 — Ultra-macro

```
Ultra-macro food photograph. Extreme close-up on a single cluster of parmesan fragments resting on a slice of Maillard-crusted roasted chicken. The parmesan granules in HYPER-DETAIL: tiny irregular powdery fragments with sandy friable texture, edges crumbling, some slightly golden and glossy from contact with hot chicken surface (but still GRANULAR, not melted, not liquid). The chicken's Maillard crust underneath: deep golden-brown caramelized surface with visible pores, ridges, micro-craters, rough bark-like texture. A single thin thread of yellow-orange pepper sauce crosses the frame diagonally, glossy and viscous, catching a bright specular highlight. A few golden sesame seeds rest soft and out-of-focus in the far background. Depth of field MINIMAL (~1-2cm sharp). Warm light from upper-left, intimate and moody. The textures are TACTILE — you can almost feel the sandy parmesan and the rough crust. ASMR visual sensation. Dark matte background. No text, no logos, no people. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```
